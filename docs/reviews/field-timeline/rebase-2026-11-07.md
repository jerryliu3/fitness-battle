The owner wants both time and course navigation directly available, without
switching modes or opening settings. Three concepts are under comparison:

- Stacked dock: full-width time and course rails inside one sheet. Largest drag
  range and explicit labels; a taller minimized sheet covers more map.
- Time header: persistent time rail under Back, with the current course/gear row
  in the sheet. Controls have different locations; time is farther from the thumb.
- Split dock: time and course rails in two columns above standings. More compact,
  with shorter drag ranges and less room for timestamps.

All concepts keep the map geometry fixed as sheet coverage changes. Time updates
historical athlete positions and standings; course navigation changes the camera
only. Course seeking remains bounded by the earned leader at the selected time.
Reduced Motion and separate gesture ownership for the sheet handle and sliders
remain implementation requirements.

An eventual integration should own one timeline state above the retained field
and standings sheet. The current route renders separate CompetitionField
instances for the map and standings, plus an independently computed overview;
independent replay cursors would make these disagree. Subscribe only while the
immersive event is visible. Snapshot cards should continue to show live totals.
