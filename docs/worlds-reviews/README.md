# Product-world review evidence

The desktop captures use a 1440 px viewport; phone captures use 390 px. All five concepts were also checked at 320 px. They use the production Next.js build with actual bundled fonts and responsive optimized mascot assets.

- `flip-*`: inked comic, selected frame, cumulative total, personal comparison, chapters and activity credits.
- `rally-*`: broadcast, same-cutoff matchup, distance field, commentary and finite replay controls.
- `roam-*`: fictional landscape, calibrated horizontal distance positions, camera controls, optional company and journal.
- `loop-*`: the new Luma character, tactile distance orbit, accessible dial, activity objects and selected receipt entry.
- `tempo-*`: album cover, A/B tape comparison, distance ribbons and activity tracks.

`verification.json` records the layout checks and interaction coverage. Roam's traveller elements intentionally clip at camera boundaries; Rally's markers extend beyond their inner track while remaining within the padded field. These intentional spatial marks are excluded from text-overflow checks and reviewed visually.

The captured week is an illustrative Thursday snapshot in Oct 12–19, 2026 UTC, rather than live tracker data. Original generated PNGs and the exact prompts are in `apps/web/public/design/worlds/` and `docs/worlds-mascot-prompts.json`.
